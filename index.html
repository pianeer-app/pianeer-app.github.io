Pianeer

Piano sight-reading practice

Pianeer is a practice tool for beginner and advanced piano players alike to learn sight-reading, improve technique and learn any new piece through user imported scores.

Coming soon to the App Store Screenshots

iPhone and iPad. Requires iOS 18.

Features

Guided Lessons
60 lessons of varying difficulty with 3-star achievements.
Song Practice
50 bundled well known pieces.
Your own music
Import any MIDI or MusicXML file.
Randomized Practice
Endless sight-reading of notes and chords in the range and clefs you set.
Drills
38 Hanon exercises for fingering technique practice.
Scale Practice
All twelve keys with one hand or both.
Chord Reference
Notation and keyboard side by side reference.
Statistics
Track your accuracy and pace over time for each practice mode.
