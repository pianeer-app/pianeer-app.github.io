Privacy Policy

Covers the Pianeer app for iPhone and iPad · Last updated 8 September 2026

What the app stores

Pianeer keeps your settings, your lesson progress, your practice history and any music you import on your device.

There is no account to create, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code of any kind. The app does not use the Advertising Identifier and cannot track you across other apps or websites.

Bluetooth and MIDI

Bluetooth is requested for one purpose: opening Apple’s standard pairing screen so you can connect a wireless MIDI keyboard. Decline it and the app still works over USB or with the on-screen piano.

Purchases

Pianeer Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple. Apple’s handling of the transaction is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.
